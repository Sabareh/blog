In this article I write about the different cloud computing services and the briefly about the service models.

# Service Models
- ***Infastructre as a Service (IaaS)***
- ***Platform as a Service (PaaS)***
- ***Software as a Service (SaaS)*** 


# Similarities of the three cloud computing models
- Customers rent them instead of buying them, shifting IT services expense from an organisation's capital expense to operational expenses.
- Vendors are rensposible for the maintenance, administration, capacity planning, trouble shooting and making backups of their system.
- Fast and easy to obtain more computing resources and scale the IT serviceds in the organisation. 

# **Infastructre as a Service**
>- Here cloud computing offer remotely accessible servers, networks, and storage capacity.
>- It is also known as ***hardware as a service***
>- The computing resources are on demand from the pool of resources that are  in their data centers.
>- IaaS customers are often tech companies with IT expertise. They want access to computing power but do no want to be responsible for installing or maintaining it. Companies like these use the infastructre available in the cloud to run software or simply store the data.
